# Mohammad Reza Servati

**Applied Cryptography, Authentication Protocols, and AI Security Researcher**

## About Me

I am a researcher specializing in applied cryptography, authentication protocols, and secure communication systems, with a particular focus on lightweight authentication and key agreement schemes for IoT, RFID, WBANs, and other resource-constrained environments.

I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering, where my thesis was recognized as the **Best M.Sc. Thesis in Cryptography (National Award)** by the Iranian Society of Cryptology.

My research has resulted in **6 peer-reviewed international journal publications**, including papers published in:

- IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management
- Computer Networks (Elsevier)
- Pervasive and Mobile Computing (Elsevier)
- IEEE Access
- Mathematics (MDPI)

My publications have received **121 Google Scholar citations** with an **h-index of 4**. I have also served as a reviewer for **21 peer-reviewed manuscripts** in leading international journals published by **Elsevier**, **Springer Nature**, and **Nature Portfolio**.

Beyond academic research, I am passionate about software engineering and technology education. I am the Co-Founder of **Servati Academy**, an educational platform focused on programming, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and software engineering. I am also the Co-Founder of **KianaLife**, where we develop technology-driven software solutions and digital products.
